## What exists today (read only)
- `LoginPage` ("STAR BPA portal") with `LoginForm`: fields Username and Password, both `required`, button "Log in"; `useLogin` maps 401 to "Wrong username or password", 503 to "Login is not available right now; try again later", any other to "Login failed (status)".
- `Header`: title, "Logged in as <user>", the group list and a "Log out" button. `MainLayout` is a header grid with the Sidebar and an `Outlet`; `/` is `TasksPage`.
- `AuthContext`: `/me` once at mount (also gives the `XSRF-TOKEN` cookie); login and logout clear the query cache. No handling of a 401 elsewhere.
- The backend already answers 401 for an unknown user or a wrong password and 400 for a missing field (`AuthApiTest`).

## Fidelity rule (Ahsan, 27 Nov: "we need exactly the same as the ui-design files")
Every screen this story builds must match its template: `01-Sign-in.html` for Sign in, the top bar and page header of `03-Personas-all.html` for the shell and the Dashboard landing. The exact values (sizes, spacing, colours, radii, shadows, fonts, icon paths, states, phone behaviour at 640 px) are in `docs/ui-screen-map.md` section 11; the tokens are in section 10. The only differences allowed are in section 11.5 (no "Test accounts" select and a real password check; the user comes from the session; no persona switcher; real data, not demo data). The visual check below is the acceptance test, not a courtesy.

## What changes
1. **Sign-in screen** (`LoginPage`, `LoginForm`, `useLogin`):
   - title and button "Sign in" (Jira), fields Username and Password, no browser bubbles (`noValidate`);
   - an empty username gives "Enter your username" and `aria-invalid="true"` on the field; an empty password gives "Enter your password"; neither posts;
   - a 401 gives "Username or password is incorrect" and the form stays;
   - 503 and other errors keep BPA-43's texts (ASSUMPTION, Q7);
   - no test-account list and no accepted-any-password behaviour (U-D90, ADR 0005).
2. **TopBar** (replaces `layout/Header`; the AppShell part is a proposal for Faraz), exactly as `docs/ui-screen-map.md` 11.2: the menu button (phone), the search with "⌘ K", and the icon buttons "Notifications" and "Logout" (Jira: "Logout"; Logout clears the session and the cache, as today, and shows Sign in with an empty password). **The "Dark mode" button is built here** (decision of Ahsan, 27 Nov: dark mode is wanted, no story): exactly as `docs/ui-screen-map.md` 11.2 and 12.1; white by default, dark only after a click on the icon, nothing remembered. **The search and its "⌘ K" hint are left out** (Q22 closed by Ahsan, 27 Nov; my reading of "leave the second"): the one visible difference from the template until the search is built. The logo "STAR" is in the sidebar in the template; until BPA-126 builds it, the top bar of this story shows the logo (ASSUMPTION).
3. **Dashboard landing** (new `pages/DashboardPage` at `/`, new `common/PageHeader`): h1 "Dashboard", subtitle "<Role> · <Department>" (names from a constants map; the source is the prototype data, ASSUMPTION, Q7 and Q18). Today's task list and panels stay under it until BPA-126 and 127 move them (Q14). No dashboard sections: those are BPA-127's.
4. **Tokens and fonts (decision of Ahsan, 27 Nov: colours and everything else come from `ui-design/`):** BPA-125 creates the design tokens in `global.css` from `docs/ui-screen-map.md` section 10 (colours by role, the 18/24 px type scale, radii, shadows, spacing, the 2 px #289CC1 focus ring), replacing the BPA-23 palette (Barlow, marking-paint yellow focus). Fonts: Manrope (headings) and Nunito Sans (text), self-hosted with `@fontsource/manrope` and `@fontsource/nunito-sans` (ADR 0017: no external requests), and **only those two** (decision of Ahsan, 27 Nov): headings `Manrope, sans-serif`, text `'Nunito Sans', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`. Telegraf and Avenir Next are not used. The token names are ours (the prototype has none). The ADR 0017 amendment is Q6.
5. No backend, contract or BPMN change.

## Risks
- The label changes break existing tests and the demo script: they are edited, listed in the PR, and the demo script is checked.
- The Jira usernames are mapped to the eleven personas in tests only (Q3); the Gherkin is not changed here.
- The landing is thin until BPA-127; the task list under it is today's.
- The tokens come from an export that the README says can differ from the live prototype in fonts and small details, and nobody has compared them (human gap); Khitaba confirms the font and the status colours against U-D28 and the component guide when they reach the repo (Q1, Q2). A change then is a token edit, not a rework.
- Replacing the palette and the font changes every existing screen's look at once (Epic 1 and 2 screens, Work Pack list and detail, task forms). Labels, roles and element structure do not change, so the existing tests keep passing; the visual check covers the old screens too.
- Until the search is built the top bar has no search: the one visible difference from the template.
- Dark mode is a CSS `filter` on the app root: a filter makes `position: fixed` descendants (the phone drawer, popovers) position against the root, not the screen. The template lives with it; the visual check at 390 px covers dark mode too.
- Dark mode and the restyle have no Jira story, so Jira shows no trace of them; the PR description and this plan are the record (Q24).
- With no automated visual test until the project is complete, "exactly the same" is checked by eye: the human check must be recorded in each PR.
- The 401-redirect on a session that expires is not covered (Q17).
